📐 Important Percentage Formulas


1. Basic Percentage

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

2. Percentage of a Number

x% of y = (x ÷ 100) × y

3. Percentage Increase

Increase % = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

4. Percentage Decrease

Decrease % = ((Original − New) ÷ Original) × 100

5. Profit Percentage

Profit % = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

6. Loss Percentage

Loss % = (Loss ÷ Cost Price) × 100

7. Discount

Discount = (Discount % ÷ 100) × Marked Price

8. Price After Discount

Final Price = Marked Price − Discount

9. Reverse Percentage

Original = Known Value ÷ (Percentage ÷ 100)

10. Percentage Difference

Difference % = |A − B| ÷ ((A+B) ÷ 2) × 100
