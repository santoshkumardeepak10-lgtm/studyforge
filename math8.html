Rational Numbers

A rational number is a number that can be written in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

📌 Important Points

  • Every integer is a rational number.
  • Denominator can never be zero.
  • Rational numbers can be positive or negative.
  • They can be compared using a common denominator.

🧠 Remember

  • a/1 = a
  • 0/a = 0, when a ≠ 0
  • Same denominator → compare numerators.
Example:

3/4 + 1/4 = 4/4 = 1

02

Linear Equations

A linear equation contains a variable whose highest power is 1. Our goal is to find the value of the variable.

📌 Method

  • Simplify both sides.
  • Bring variable terms together.
  • Bring constant terms together.
  • Find the value of the variable.

🧠 Golden Rule

  • Whatever operation you perform on one side, perform it on the other side.
  • Keep the equation balanced.
Example:

2x + 5 = 15
2x = 10
x = 5

Squares & Square Roots

The square of a number is obtained by multiplying the number by itself.

📌 Important

  • 5² = 5 × 5 = 25
  • 10² = 100
  • Perfect squares have whole-number square roots.

🧠 Useful Pattern

  • 1² = 1
  • 2² = 4
  • 3² = 9
  • 4² = 16
  • 5² = 25
Example:

√144 = 12 because 12 × 12 = 144.

Cubes & Cube Roots

The cube of a number is obtained by multiplying it three times by itself.

📌 Formula

  • a³ = a × a × a
  • 2³ = 8
  • 3³ = 27
  • 4³ = 64

🧠 Cube Root

  • ∛8 = 2
  • ∛27 = 3
  • ∛64 = 4
Example:

5³ = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

Percentage

Percentage means "per hundred". It is commonly used to compare quantities and calculate changes.

📌 Formula

  • Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
  • Profit = SP − CP
  • Loss = CP − SP

🧠 Remember

  • 25% = 25/100 = 1/4
  • 50% = 1/2
  • 100% = whole quantity
Example:

20 out of 100 = 20%.
Therefore, 20% of 200 = 40.

PROOFS & THEOREMS

Important Proofs & Properties

Learn important Class 8 geometry results with proper reasoning instead of simply memorising them.

01

Linear Pair of Angles

📌 Statement

If two adjacent angles form a straight line, then their sum is 180°.

Given:

∠AOB and ∠BOC form a linear pair.


To Prove:

∠AOB + ∠BOC = 180°

🧠 Proof

Since A, O and C lie on the same straight line, ∠AOC is a straight angle.

Therefore,

∠AOC = 180°

Also,

∠AOB + ∠BOC = ∠AOC

Therefore,

∠AOB + ∠BOC = 180°

✅ Conclusion

Hence, the sum of angles forming a linear pair is always 180°.

Hence proved.

02

Vertically Opposite Angles

Statement:

When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are equal.

🧠 Proof

Suppose two lines AB and CD intersect at O.

∠AOC and ∠AOD form a linear pair.

Therefore:

∠AOC + ∠AOD = 180°

Similarly:

∠AOD + ∠DOB = 180°

Therefore:

∠AOC + ∠AOD = ∠AOD + ∠DOB

Subtracting ∠AOD from both sides:

∠AOC = ∠DOB

Hence, vertically opposite angles are equal.

03

Angle Sum Property of a Triangle

Statement:

The sum of the three interior angles of a triangle is 180°.

Given:

ΔABC


To Prove:

∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°

📐 Construction

Draw a line through A parallel to BC.

Let this line be XY.

🧠 Proof

Since XY ∥ BC, alternate interior angles are equal.

Therefore:

∠XAB = ∠ABC

and

∠CAY = ∠ACB

Now XAY is a straight line.

Therefore:

∠XAB + ∠BAC + ∠CAY = 180°

Substituting:

∠ABC + ∠BAC + ∠ACB = 180°

Therefore:

∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°

Hence proved.

04

Exterior Angle Property of a Triangle

Statement:

An exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of its two opposite interior angles.

🧠 Proof

Consider ΔABC and extend BC to D.

∠ACD is an exterior angle.

From the angle-sum property:

∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°

Since ∠ACD and ∠C form a linear pair:

∠ACD + ∠C = 180°

Comparing both equations:

∠ACD = ∠A + ∠B

Hence proved.

05

Angle Sum Property of a Quadrilateral

Statement:

The sum of all interior angles of a quadrilateral is 360°.

🧠 Proof

Consider quadrilateral ABCD.

Draw diagonal AC.

The quadrilateral is divided into two triangles:

ΔABC and ΔACD.

Sum of angles of ΔABC = 180°

Sum of angles of ΔACD = 180°

Therefore:

180° + 180° = 360°

Hence:

∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°

Hence proved.

06

Opposite Angles of a Parallelogram

Statement:

Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal.

🧠 Proof

Let ABCD be a parallelogram.

Since AB ∥ CD and AD ∥ BC, consecutive interior angles are supplementary.

Therefore:

∠A + ∠B = 180°

Also:

∠B + ∠C = 180°

Therefore:

∠A + ∠B = ∠B + ∠C

Subtracting ∠B:

∠A = ∠C

Similarly:

∠B = ∠D

Hence opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal.

07

Opposite Sides of a Parallelogram

Statement:

Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.

🧠 Proof

Let ABCD be a parallelogram. Draw diagonal AC.

Since AB ∥ CD:

∠BAC = ∠DCA

Since AD ∥ BC:

∠BCA = ∠DAC

AC is common to both triangles.

Therefore:

ΔABC ≅ ΔCDA by ASA.

Hence corresponding sides are equal:

AB = CD

and

BC = AD

Hence proved.

08

Diagonals of a Parallelogram

Statement:

The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.

🧠 Proof

Let ABCD be a parallelogram and let diagonals AC and BD intersect at O.

Since AB ∥ CD:

∠ABO = ∠CDO

Since AB ∥ CD:

∠BAO = ∠DCO

Also:

AB = CD

Therefore:

ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOD

Hence corresponding parts are equal:

AO = OC

and

BO = OD

Therefore the diagonals bisect each other.

Hence proved.

09

Sum of Interior Angles of a Polygon

Formula:

Sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon = (n − 2) × 180°

🧠 Why?

From one vertex of a polygon, draw diagonals to all non-adjacent vertices.

An n-sided polygon gets divided into (n − 2) triangles.

Each triangle has angle sum 180°.

Therefore:

Sum = (n − 2) × 180°

10

Equal Sides and Equal Angles

Statement:

In an isosceles triangle, angles opposite equal sides are equal.

🧠 Proof Idea

Consider ΔABC where AB = AC.

Since the two sides are equal, the triangle has symmetry about the angle bisector from A.

The two smaller triangles formed by this bisector have corresponding equal sides and a common side.

Therefore the corresponding base angles are equal:

∠B = ∠C

Hence proved.

08

Mensuration

Mensuration is the study of measurement of length, perimeter, area and other quantities related to shapes.

📌 Rectangle

  • Area = l × b
  • Perimeter = 2(l + b)

📌 Square

  • Area = side²
  • Perimeter = 4 × side
Example:

Rectangle with length 8 cm and breadth 5 cm:
Area = 8 × 5 = 40 cm².
