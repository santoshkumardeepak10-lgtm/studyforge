Physics Formula Library

Important Physics formulas for Class 8, 9 and 10. Learn formulas topic-wise and use them in numerical problems.

🚗 Motion

Speed

v = d / t

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

v = speed, d = distance, t = time

Distance

d = v × t

Distance = Speed × Time

Time

t = d / v

Time = Distance ÷ Speed

Average Speed

Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time

Velocity

v = Displacement / Time

Acceleration

a = (v - u) / t

u = initial velocity

v = final velocity

Final Velocity

v = u + at

First Equation of Motion

v = u + at

Second Equation of Motion

s = ut + ½at²

Third Equation of Motion

v² = u² + 2as

Displacement

s = ut + ½at²

Average Velocity

Average Velocity = Total Displacement / Total Time

💪 Force & Laws of Motion

Newton's Second Law

F = ma

Force = Mass × Acceleration

Mass

m = F / a

Acceleration

a = F / m

Momentum

p = mv

p = momentum

Change in Momentum

Δp = mv - mu

Force using Momentum

F = Δp / Δt

Weight

W = mg

g ≈ 9.8 m/s²

Impulse

Impulse = F × t

🌍 Gravitation

Universal Law of Gravitation

F = Gm₁m₂ / r²

Acceleration due to Gravity

g = GM / R²

Weight

W = mg

Mass from Weight

m = W / g

Free Fall

v = u + gt

Distance in Free Fall

s = ut + ½gt²

Free Fall Velocity

v² = u² + 2gs

Density

ρ = m / V

Mass from Density

m = ρV

Volume

V = m / ρ

Pressure

P = F / A

Force from Pressure

F = PA

⚡ Work, Energy & Power

Work

W = Fs

When force and displacement are in same direction.

Work at an Angle

W = Fs cos θ

Kinetic Energy

KE = ½mv²

Potential Energy

PE = mgh

Mechanical Energy

ME = KE + PE

Power

P = W / t

Work

W = Pt

Power using Force and Velocity

P = Fv

Efficiency

Efficiency = (Useful Output / Input) × 100

Energy Conversion

Input Energy = Output Energy + Energy Loss

🧱 Pressure

Pressure

P = F / A

Force

F = PA

Area

A = F / P

Liquid Pressure

P = ρgh

Atmospheric Pressure

P = F / A

🔥 Heat & Temperature

Heat Energy

Q = mcΔT

m = mass, c = specific heat

Specific Heat Capacity

c = Q / (mΔT)

Temperature Change

ΔT = T₂ - T₁

Latent Heat

Q = mL

Specific Latent Heat

L = Q / m

Celsius to Fahrenheit

°F = (9/5 × °C) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius

°C = 5/9 × (°F - 32)

Celsius to Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

Kelvin to Celsius

°C = K - 273.15

🔊 Waves & Sound

Wave Speed

v = fλ

f = frequency, λ = wavelength

Frequency

f = v / λ

Wavelength

λ = v / f

Frequency

f = 1 / T

Time Period

T = 1 / f

Number of Vibrations

f = n / t

Echo Distance

d = vt / 2

For an echo returning to the source.

💡 Light

Speed of Light

c ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s

Mirror Formula

1/f = 1/v + 1/u

Magnification

m = hᵢ / hₒ

Mirror Magnification

m = -v / u

Lens Formula

1/f = 1/v - 1/u

Lens Magnification

m = v / u

Power of Lens

P = 1 / f

f must be in metres.

Focal Length

f = 1 / P

Power of Combined Lenses

P = P₁ + P₂ + P₃ + ...

Refractive Index

n = c / v

Snell's Law

n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r

Refractive Index using Angles

n = sin i / sin r

Electrical Energy

Resistivity

R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃

Parallel Resistance
